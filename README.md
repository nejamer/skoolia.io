# Skoolia — School Management System & AI Timetable Generator

**Skoolia** is a cloud-based school management system for private and international K-12 schools. It brings student records, admissions, attendance, timetabling, the gradebook, fees and parent communication into one platform, with **Najiba**, a built-in AI assistant that answers questions from your school's own data.

🌐 Official website: **[https://skoolia.com](https://skoolia.com)**

> This repository is a public product overview. It contains no application source code.

## Built for international schools

- **Four interface languages**: English, French, Arabic (full right-to-left layout) and Spanish.
- **Grading systems**: IB, Cambridge and A-Level, US, French, Canadian and Irish, plus custom grading scales.
- **Data import**: students, staff, classes, subjects and facilities from CSV or Excel, with a template for each type.
- **Guided onboarding** on every school plan, covering migration, staff training and the parent rollout. Most schools go live within two to six weeks.

## Security and data privacy

- Each school's data lives in its own isolated tenant with role-based access for administrators, teachers, parents and students.
- Your school's data is **not used to train AI models**. Najiba sends only the data needed to answer a question, scoped to your organisation.
- Sensitive actions are recorded in an audit trail.
- A data processing agreement is available on request.
